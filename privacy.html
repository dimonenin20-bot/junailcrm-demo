Privacy Policy — ThredsApi (JuNailCRM)

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ThredsApi (part of the JuNailCRM studio automation suite) collects, processes, and protects data accessed through the Meta Threads API.

1. Information We Access

When an authorized user grants permissions via Meta Threads OAuth, the application accesses only the minimum data needed to operate:

  • Profile Basics (threads_basic): Threads user ID and public username.
  • Content Publishing (threads_content_publish): Publishing studio announcements and replies authorized by the account owner.
  • Reply Management (threads_read_replies, threads_manage_replies): Reading and responding to comments on the account owner's posts.
  • Public Keyword Discovery (threads_keyword_search): Searching public Threads posts containing beauty/manicure service keywords to surface relevant industry discussions and leads.

2. How Data Is Used

  • To publish scheduled studio posts and respond to comments on behalf of the authorized account owner.
  • To display relevant public Threads posts and usernames to the authorized operator in a private management interface.
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer Threads data to third-party data brokers or ad networks.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Authorized access tokens and operational logs are stored securely on a private server accessible only to the application owner. Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide active workflow automation or until deletion is requested.

4. User Data Deletion & Revocation

Users may revoke access at any time via Threads Settings → Account → Website Permissions, or request complete deletion of stored records by following our Data Deletion Instructions.
